# India Luxury Car Market Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive 5-page Power BI dashboard analyzing India's luxury automobile market across 14 brands. The dashboard provides insights into market trends, brand competition, state-wise registrations, model performance, and luxury EV adoption.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze the luxury automobile market and answer key business questions:

- How is the luxury car market changing over time?
- Which brands dominate the market?
- Which states have the highest luxury vehicle registrations?
- Which models and vehicle types are most popular?
- How is luxury EV adoption developing?
- How do luxury brands compare in overall vehicle sales versus EV sales?

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
Provides a high-level view of the luxury automobile market, including:

- Overall market trends
- Brand performance
- Top luxury brands
- Market distribution

### 2. States
Analyzes the geographic distribution of luxury vehicle registrations across Indian states.

- State-wise luxury registrations
- Geographic distribution
- State contribution to luxury registrations

### 3. Brands
Compares luxury automobile brands based on:

- Vehicle sales
- Market share
- Brand rankings
- Competitive performance

### 4. Models
Analyzes luxury vehicle models and segments:

- Top-performing models
- Brand and model mix
- SUV vs Sedan distribution
- Model-level performance

### 5. EV Analysis
Focuses on luxury electric vehicle adoption:

- Luxury EV sales
- EV share
- EV sales by brand
- Brand EV positioning

- Total vehicle sales
- Market share
- Brand rankings
- Top-performing brands
- Previous-period sales
- Sales growth
- Luxury EV sales
- EV share
- Top-performing models

## 📈 Key Insights

- Mercedes-Benz and BMW-Mini are the leading luxury brands in FY2024 based on the analyzed sales data.
- Maharashtra has the highest cumulative luxury vehicle registrations among the states covered.
- SUVs account for a larger share of luxury model sales than sedans in the FY2023 model dataset.
- BMW leads the luxury EV brands in the FY2024 EV dataset.
- Luxury EV adoption increased significantly from FY2023 to FY2024 in the available data.
